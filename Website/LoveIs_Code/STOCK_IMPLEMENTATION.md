# Stock & ton kho - cong viec trien khai

## Da trien khai
- Quan ly ton kho theo bien the (StockQty) trong public flow.
- Chan them vao gio neu het hang hoac vuot ton kho.
- Kiem tra ton kho khi dat hang, neu thieu hang thi bao loi.
- Tru kho khi dat hang thanh cong.
- Hoan kho khi huy don hoac gui yeu cau doi/tra.
- Hien thi trang thai con/hết hang o trang chi tiet san pham va gio hang.
